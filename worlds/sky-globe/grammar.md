# Sky Globe: layout grammar

- One focal point per screen. The hero's focal point is the globe; the headline is the second.
- Sections alternate white and pale-sky surfaces; never stack three card grids in a row.
- Cards are only for things the user compares (rates, categories); explanatory content is open text on the surface.
- Category tiles are full-bleed images (aspect 4/5), name and one line overlaid bottom-left on a navy scrim; no white block under the image.
- Process steps carry their own art-directed image, not numbered circles.
- Primary action is the one saturated blue button; secondary actions are text links.
- Headlines: Manrope 700-800, tight tracking (-0.03em), two lines at most on phones.
- Do not reintroduce a plane/blueprint motif: it was rejected four times.
