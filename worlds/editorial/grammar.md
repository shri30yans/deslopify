# Editorial Paper: layout grammar

- Text is the interface. Headlines are serif; body is a clean sans at 18-20px with a 62-70ch measure.
- Structure with hairline rules (1px, the line token) instead of boxes or cards.
- The accent colour marks one thing per screen: the italic word, the action, or a rule. Never fill large areas with it.
- Alignment is left; centred text is reserved for a single pull quote.
- Lists are numbered or dated rows separated by rules, not tiles.
- Images are large and captioned; crop them to the grid, never to rounded rectangles.
- Radius is zero everywhere, including buttons and inputs.
