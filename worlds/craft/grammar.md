# Warm Craft: layout grammar

- Surfaces are warm cream and ivory panels with 20px radius; separation comes from tone, not borders or shadows.
- Terracotta (accent) is for the primary action and one shape per screen; sand and olive are supporting tones for illustration only.
- Headlines are Fraunces 600 with natural tracking; body is Outfit at 18px / 1.6.
- Illustration is made of flat arches, suns and leaves, drawn with simple geometry in the palette. Photos, when used, are warm and uncropped to circles.
- Buttons are pill-radius, filled for the primary action and outlined for secondary.
- Keep paragraphs short; one idea each.
