# Dark Instrument: layout grammar

- Panels share borders (1px line token) and sit on the grid; no drop shadows, no glows, no gradients.
- Amber (accent) is the only saturated colour. It marks the primary action and at most one live element per screen.
- Labels, units, keys and numbers are monospace uppercase 12px with 0.06em tracking; sentences are sans.
- Density is high but never cramped: 16px minimum between text and any border.
- Data and diagrams are drawn at full contrast; muted text is for labels only, never for the main sentence.
- Radius is small (4px) on controls and zero on panels.
